The Ballot Office

Sketch for Secret Hitler Voting. Each phone is a ballot card in a dim ministry back room: buff card stock, typed rosters, brass for the one thing you should press, and a rubber stamp for the verdict. JA! and NEIN are printed on identical ballot cards, so the person in the next seat sees paper and nothing else.

Fonts: Big Shoulders Display (poster headlines), Libre Franklin (interface copy), Courier Prime (typed names and records). Hold any dashed “Hold to check” bar to try it. The result frames replay the reveal.
